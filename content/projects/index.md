# Projects

A collection of research tools, interactive visualizations, and computational projects related to oceanography and phytoplankton ecology.

## Interactive Simulations

### [NPZD Model Simulator](npzd-model)
Web-based ecosystem model with real-time parameter controls and dual visualization modes (time series and flow diagrams).

[Launch →](https://patcdaniel.github.io/npz-model/)

## Machine Learning

### [PhytoViT - Phytoplankton Image Classifier](phytovit-classifier)
A Vision Transformer fine-tuned on ~558k IFCB images to classify 83 phytoplankton taxa at 95.87% accuracy.

[Demo →](https://huggingface.co/spaces/patcdaniel/ifcbSingleClassifier)

### [Detecting Dividing Prorocentrum](dividing-prorocentrum-classifier)
A ViT model for automated detection of dividing *Prorocentrum* cells from IFCB imagery, enabling in situ division rate estimation.
